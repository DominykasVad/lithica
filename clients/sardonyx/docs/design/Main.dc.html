Description

Mira Vell has kept the Gullrock lighthouse alone for eleven winters. She is thirty-four, wiry from the stairs, with salt-cracked hands and a habit of talking to the lamp as if it could answer. She distrusts boats, keeps a ledger of every ship that passes, and has started to notice names in it that no harbour has ever heard of.
